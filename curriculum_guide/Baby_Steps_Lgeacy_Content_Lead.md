**What this pack is**

A finite assignment for the content lead. It lists every pilot-stage concept, child-facing line, answer key, recording and image brief. The lead does not invent a topic list; they approve, correct and complete named rows.

# **1\. Commission decision and boundary**

The supplied curriculum specification recommends commissioning Stages 1 and 2 first, integrating them, testing with children and caregivers across ages 3-5, 6-8 and 9-12, and only then scaling to Stages 3-10. This pack follows that boundary. It is therefore exact for the first release rather than superficially complete for all ten stages.

**Non-negotiable language status**

This is a production draft, not approved language content. The content lead must approve or replace every Luganda string, provide an independent English back-translation, and approve the final edited audio before release.

# **4\. Review method the lead must use**

- Choose and declare the intended Luganda variety and orthographic convention before approving any row.
- Review the English communicative intent first; do not mechanically translate the English wording if natural Luganda expresses it differently.
- For each row, approve or replace the Luganda, then ask another bilingual reviewer to back-translate without seeing the English source intent.
- Resolve differences for meaning, naturalness, child appropriateness, noun agreement, spelling, tone/context and cultural fit.
- Read every final line aloud. Confirm it is clear for a beginner and not patronising for an older beginner.
- Record only approved scripts. After editing, a native reviewer listens to the actual file and signs off that it matches the approved text.
- Field-test the integrated pilot separately with ages 3-5, 6-8 and 9-12 before the next content commission.

# **6\. Stage 1 - Greetings, names and polite language**

**Luganda title draft: Ennamusa, amannya n'empisa ennungi**

Recognize and use-with caregiver support-basic greetings, responses, thanks, apology, self-introduction and goodbye. The app does not score speech.

**Parent-facing copy**

Summary: This stage practises greetings, saying one's name, thanking, apologising and saying goodbye.  
<br/>Luganda follow-up draft: Mulumusagane ku makya ne ku ggulo. Buli omu yeeyanjule n'erinnya lye.  
English intent: Greet each other in the morning and later in the day. Let each person introduce themselves by name.

# **10\. What is intentionally not commissioned**

- No pronunciation score, speech recognition, voice recording, tracing, handwriting or free-text answer content: the current Learning Hub does not validly support these as measured activities.
- No automatic age gate or three complete age-band rewrites. The same beginner concepts serve ages 3-12 for the pilot; field testing determines later presentation variants.
- No broad Stages 3-10 script or audio production before the first two stages are integrated and tested.
- No audio for quiz options or general prompts unless the runtime is first updated to support and map it reliably. The 29 files in this pack are the complete current audio commission.
- No unlicensed internet imagery, copyrighted characters, generic text-to-speech, machine-translated production text or cross-language fallback.
